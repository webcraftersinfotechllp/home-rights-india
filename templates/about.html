Who We Are

About Homerights India Social Foundation

HOMERIGHTS INDIA SOCIAL FOUNDATION is a community-centred organization dedicated to empowering marginalized families and uplifting lives. Through rights awareness, sustainable development, and active grassroots engagement, we create lasting social pathways across education, rural agriculture, child rights, civic growth, and animal protection.

We believe social progress begins when people are heard, equipped, and given equitable opportunities to thrive. That is why every initiative we design is rooted in trust, meaningful participation, and practical community engagement.

Our Vision

A just, inclusive, and empowered society

We envision communities where every individual has the opportunity to live with dignity, access essential support in education, farming, civic rights, and animal welfare, and participate in collective progress without barriers.

Our Mission

To create lasting community impact

Our mission is to enable access to education & literacy, sustainable agricultural support, child welfare, civic rights awareness, and animal protection for marginalized communities through collaborative grassroots action.

Our Values

What guides us

  • Integrity
  • community-first action
  • inclusivity
  • transparency
  • deep empathy drive every initiative we undertake

Our Approach

Grounded in local realities

We combine grassroots listening with practical, field-tested frameworks to design programmes across agriculture, education, civic action, child development, and animal welfare. We work collaboratively with families, farmers, schools, youth groups, and local community stakeholders.

Why We Work

Because communities deserve access, opportunity, and voice

Many families, farmers, and neighbourhoods face exclusion from basic opportunities, quality education, rights awareness, and welfare support. We work to bridge those gaps with compassion, practical grassroots action, and sustained community engagement.

Leadership

Driven by dedicated social visionaries

HOMERIGHTS INDIA SOCIAL FOUNDATION is led by passionate social workers, community organizers, and field advisors committed to driving grassroots development and institutional integrity across all focus sectors.
