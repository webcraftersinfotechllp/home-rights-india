About Us

Creating Change. Empowering Communities.

HOMERIGHTS INDIA SOCIAL FOUNDATION is dedicated to driving grassroots change across Agriculture, Child Welfare, Civic Issues, Education & Literacy, and Animal Welfare. We work directly with local communities to implement impactful programs and events that create long-term social growth.

Mr. Milind Vasantrao Gaikwad And Mr. Sanjay Uttamrao Alhat Lead by experienced visionaries, Homerights India Social Foundation is committed to fostering equitable community growth, protecting fundamental rights, and driving sustainable grassroots development across all sectors.
