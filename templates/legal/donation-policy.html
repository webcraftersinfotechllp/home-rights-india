Donation Policy

Donations made to HOMERIGHTS INDIA SOCIAL FOUNDATION are voluntary contributions directly intended to support our grassroots programmes in education, agriculture, child welfare, civic rights, and animal protection.

As a registered non-profit with valid Section 12A and Section 80G status, all eligible donations made by Indian donors qualify for tax deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

To receive an official 80G tax-exemption receipt, donors must mandatorily provide their Permanent Account Number (PAN) and correct contact details during the donation process.

Official electronic receipts and acknowledgments are issued to donors upon successful credit verification for statutory tax compliance and transparency.
