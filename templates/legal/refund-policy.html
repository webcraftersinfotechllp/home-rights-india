Refund Policy

Donations made to HOMERIGHTS INDIA SOCIAL FOUNDATION are considered final and non-refundable once processed, as funds are immediately committed to active community programs.

Exceptions may be reviewed in cases of technical glitches, duplicate transactions, or erroneous payments made through the payment gateway.

To request a refund for a duplicate transaction, donors must email info@homerightsindia.in within 7 days with valid proof of deduction and payment ID.

Approved refunds will be processed back to the original source account after verification and compliance checks.
